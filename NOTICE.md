# NurseDesk notices

NurseDesk is an independent, unofficial study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Galen College of Nursing or ATI Nursing Education.

**No course material is included.** The installer and this repository contain no Galen or ATI slides, quizzes, practice tests or study guides. Course content only enters the app if you import files you are entitled to use (your own PowerPoints/PDFs, or a course pack from a classmate). Share a course pack only with people who are entitled to the same materials, and check your school's policy first.

**Study aid, not clinical advice.** The "General reference" text on the Medications tab, the glossary and any AI answer may contain mistakes. Never use them for patient care, doses or lab decisions. Use your course materials, your instructor and your facility's policies.

**Your data.** Progress, notes and your API key stay on your PC. Questions are sent to Anthropic only if you add your own API key. The optional built-in AI runs on your PC.

Third-party software: Electron (MIT), llama.cpp (MIT) and the Qwen model (Apache-2.0, downloaded only if you switch on the built-in AI), pdf.js (Apache-2.0), JSZip (MIT or GPL-3.0, used under MIT).
